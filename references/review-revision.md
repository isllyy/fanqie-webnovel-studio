# Review and Revision

Use this reference for 审稿, 改稿, 去AI味, single-chapter quality checks, and write-then-review.

## Review Priorities

1. Preserve the author's plot premise and confirmed story facts.
2. Improve reader immersion, emotional pull, and follow-through.
3. Strengthen protagonist agency and visible payoff.
4. Keep romance strong without stealing the protagonist's core highlight.
5. Check the chapter engine: goal, obstacle, turn, consequence, hook.
6. Remove AI-like wording, repeated patterns, empty emotion, and explanatory dialogue.
7. Tighten logic, pacing, scene purpose, and chapter-end pull.
8. Fix mobile readability: paragraphs, punctuation, dialogue attribution, long blocks.
9. For period settings, mark uncertain background details as `需查证`.

## AI-Flavor Signals

Fix these before delivery:

- Over-neat sentence patterns and symmetrical contrasts.
- "不是X而是Y"/"并非X，而是Y" used repeatedly.
- Empty emotional labels: "心里五味杂陈", "一丝情绪涌上心头".
- Stock actions: "深吸一口气", "眼中闪过一丝", "嘴角勾起一抹".
- Formulaic atmosphere: "空气仿佛凝固".
- Narrator explainers: "她不知道的是", "之所以...是因为".
- Dialogue that explains setting instead of pressuring or revealing character.
- Endings that summarize or elevate instead of landing on action, line, danger, or reveal.

Suggested hard limit for opening chapters: `不是X而是Y` no more than once per chapter; preferably zero when a direct sentence works.

## Natural Prose Targets

- Paragraph length varies with tension.
- Concrete objects and actions carry emotion.
- Dialogue tags are low-frequency and not formulaic.
- Characters have distinct speaking habits.
- Exposition is embedded into conflict, action, or discovery.
- The ending leaves next-step pull.

## Fanqie Female-Fiction Quality

Check:

- Does the opening enter pressure quickly?
- Does the heroine make a meaningful choice or judgment?
- Does the chapter change situation, relationship, secret, pressure, or reader expectation?
- Is there payoff or a clear promise of payoff?
- Is the male lead useful without replacing the heroine's agency?
- Are relationship beats test, trust, leverage, protection, debt, or secret rather than instant sweetness?

## Historical / Period Setting Check

For 民国, 年代, 抗战, historical, or period fiction, check:

- Modern internet phrasing.
- Modern relationship logic that breaks the era.
- Titles, address forms, clothing, transport, weaponry, communications, medicine, currency, food, customs, and organizations.
- Geographic or military claims affecting plot logic.

If uncertain, mark `需查证` and avoid a hard claim.

## Rewrite Levels

Choose the smallest effective repair:

1. Line polish.
2. Paragraph rewrite.
3. Scene rebuild.
4. Chapter beat rebuild.
5. Full chapter rewrite.

For minor issues, provide replacements.
For medium issues, rewrite affected paragraphs.
For major issues, provide a beat rebuild and revised正文.

## Before Delivery

Do not deliver knowingly weak draft prose. Confirm:

- Opening has tension, conflict, or a question.
- Protagonist acts or prepares to act.
- At least one emotional or plot turn exists.
- Pure exposition is broken by action/dialogue/pressure/detail.
- Ending pulls into the next chapter.
- No new continuity or setting contradictions are introduced.
