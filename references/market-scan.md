# Market Scan

Use this reference when the user asks what to write, what is hot, or wants榜单/扫榜 analysis.

## Principle

Scan for repeatable patterns, not isolated hits. A single top-ranked book is not enough; multiple samples in the same direction are a stronger signal.

If live data is unavailable, say the conclusion is based on prior or general market knowledge and recommend validating with current榜单 before committing.

## What to Analyze

1. Topic distribution: how many books per genre/subgenre.
2. New-book signals: what directions are currently emerging.
3. Cross-list repetition: themes appearing across several lists.
4. Title patterns: words, sentence forms, promise clarity.
5. Length expectations: word count range in the lane.
6. Tag language: intro tags reveal real reader segmentation.

## Feasibility

- High: repeated samples, stable or rising trend, author material supports it, clear differentiation.
- Medium: samples exist but trend/differentiation/material is uncertain.
- Low: saturated lane, weak differentiation, poor platform fit, or material cannot sustain a serial.

When recommending without live榜单, cap confidence at medium.
