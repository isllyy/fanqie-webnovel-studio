# Chapter Pipeline

Use this reference for 写第N章, 续写, 日更, 马拉松模式, and chapter handoff.

## Prepare

Before writing a chapter:

1. Read `04-追踪/上下文.md` if it exists.
2. Read `00-项目/作者偏好.md` and `00-项目/项目设定总表.md` if they exist.
3. Read the chapter outline under `02-大纲/细纲/`.
4. Note word count, must-happen beats, forbidden beats, chapter-end hook type, active promises, and hidden truths.
5. If the chapter outline is missing or contradictory, stop and create/confirm it first.

## Draft

- Expand the confirmed chapter outline.
- Show rather than explain: use action, dialogue, concrete objects, and consequences.
- Every chapter needs conflict, turn, relationship movement, or new information pressure.
- Keep mobile reading in mind: short-to-medium paragraphs, varied rhythm.
- Dialogue should reveal pressure, power, relationship, or concealment. Avoid exposition disguised as dialogue.

New content policy:

- Directly write: passersby, one-time objects, small place details, one-time dialogue.
- Write and track: named supporting characters, recurring locations, rules, foreshadowing, promises.
- Ask first: new main reversal, new power rule, hidden-truth reveal, changed confirmed outcome.

## Self-Review

After drafting, check:

- Word count is within target.
- Opening connects to previous hook and does not over-recap.
- Chapter-end hook matches outline and recent hooks are varied.
- No placeholders, inconsistent names, or meta words like "本章/作者/读者".
- AI-flavor check passes or is revised with `review-revision.md`.
- Names, places, titles, timeline, hidden truths, and reader-known facts match tracking.
- The chapter's promised payoff is visible.

## Finalize and Track

Deliver or save the revised final正文.

Then update:

- `04-追踪/上下文.md`: position, active foreshadowing, character state, next promise, risks, hidden truths.
- `04-追踪/伏笔.md`: new/recovered/retired foreshadowing.
- `04-追踪/逐章记录.md`: facts introduced this chapter.

Report only:

- What was written or changed.
- What needs the author's decision.
- Next step.

## Three-Chapter Loop

After every 3 written chapters:

- Check each chapter against self-review.
- Confirm at least one payoff in the 3-chapter span.
- Confirm hook types are not repetitive.
- Check whether the same setting has been used too long.
- Reconcile tracking files with正文.
- Record repeated style problems into author preferences.

## Marathon Mode

Only use when the user clearly asks for continuous writing. Even then:

- Keep a maximum of 3 chapters per batch.
- Do not skip chapter self-review or tracking updates.
- Stop if author approval is needed, a confirmed outline ends, or a blocking contradiction appears.
